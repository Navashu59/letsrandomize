Contact LetsRandomize

Use this page for feedback about LetsRandomize tools, bug reports, privacy questions, correction requests, and suggestions for new random generators.

Email

General feedback and tool suggestions: hello@letsrandomize.org

Privacy questions: privacy@letsrandomize.org

Terms or policy questions: terms@letsrandomize.org

What to include

For bug reports, include the tool URL, your browser and device, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not send private lists, passwords, student rosters, or other sensitive data.

For content corrections, include the page URL and the sentence or section that needs review. Methodology questions are best sent with the tool name and the use case you are trying to validate.

Response notes

LetsRandomize is a small independent site, so response times vary. Privacy and security-related messages are prioritized. For common questions about how the tools work, see the methodology page and the privacy policy.
