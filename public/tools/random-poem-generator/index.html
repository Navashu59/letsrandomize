Poem Styles Explained

Haiku (5‑7‑5)

A traditional Japanese form with three lines of 5, 7, and 5 syllables respectively. Haiku often focus on nature, seasons, and a moment of awareness.

Rhyming Couplet

Two lines that rhyme and share the same meter. Couplets are concise and can be humorous, reflective, or narrative.

Free Verse

Poetry without a fixed rhyme or meter. Free verse uses natural speech rhythms and line breaks to create its musicality.

Classic Style

Inspired by traditional English poetry, with regular meter and often a formal tone. Suitable for themes of love, time, and reflection.
