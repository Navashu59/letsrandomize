Spin the Wheel - Random Wheel Spinner

Reviewed by the LetsRandomize Editorial Team|Updated Mar 4, 2026

Add your names or choices, tap Spin, and the wheel picks a random winner. Uses cryptographic randomness for fair results. Customize colors, remove winners after each spin, or add up to 100 options. Works for classrooms, giveaways, team picks, and any decision you need to make.

How to Use the Wheel Spinner

1

Type Your Choices

Enter your options in the text box, one per line. The wheel updates instantly as you type. You can add up to 100 choices.

2

Click the Spin Button

Press "Spin the Wheel!" or click the center of the wheel. The wheel spins with a smooth, satisfying animation.

3

Wait for the Result

The wheel gradually slows down and lands on a random choice. A ticking sound plays as it spins.

4

See the Winner

The winning choice is highlighted and announced. Copy the result, share it, or spin again for a new pick.

When a wheel is the right random picker

Use the wheel when the random choice is shared with a group and the visible spin helps everyone follow the result. It works well for classroom turns, party prompts, agenda choices, and casual giveaways where the reveal is part of the activity.

If you need a copied ordered list, use the list randomizer. If you need teams, use the team generator. The wheel is for one visible pick at a time; formal drawings should still follow official rules and recordkeeping. See the methodology page for randomization limits.
