Randomness & Probability Guides

Whether you're running a classroom lottery, building a secure password, or curious about how random number generators actually work, our guides explain the science and practice behind randomness. Each guide includes interactive tools so you can apply what you learn immediately.

Why Understanding Randomness Matters

Random selection affects everyday decisions: picking contest winners, generating passwords, splitting teams, and making fair choices. Poorly implemented randomness leads to bias, predictable passwords, and unfair outcomes. Our guides cover the fundamentals — from coin flip probability to cryptographic random number generation — so you can make informed decisions about fairness, security, and chance.
